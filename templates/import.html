How to export from Prospera
  1. Sign in to Prospera Business Online Banking.
  2. Open the account and choose the option to download or export the account activity.
  3. Choose a date range. Choose OFX, QFX (Quicken), or QBO (QuickBooks) if offered: these carry the bank's own transaction IDs. Otherwise choose CSV.
  4. Upload the file here. For a CSV, check the column choices once. The app remembers them for that account.

Prospera plans to move to the Coast Capital banking platform. The export layout may change then. If it does, adjust the column choices on the preview page.
