Reports

Each report opens as a PDF that you can print, email, or file.

Monthly treasurer's report

For each PAC meeting. Bank balances with a check against the bank, budget against actual for the month and the year to date, every transaction in the month, and items to resolve.

Annual report for the AGM

Opening and closing balance of each fund, budget against actual with variances, bank balances at year end, monthly totals, the gaming grant summary, your notes, and signature lines.

Add notes for the members on the budget page under "Fiscal year".

Budget proposal

Next year's proposed budget beside last year's budget and actuals, with the change for each line and an approval block. Edit the amounts on the proposal page first.
