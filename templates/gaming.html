What gaming funds can and cannot pay for (PAC rules, 2026)

Eligible (examples)

  • Field trips within BC
  • Presentations and performances
  • School spirit and family events (no fundraising)
  • Leisure items for non-instructional time
  • Beautification of common spaces
  • Yearbooks and newsletters
  • Student competitions and clubs
  • Graduation and dry grad
  • Team uniforms and sports equipment
  • Playground equipment
  • Sports day costs
  • Student recognition
  • Student conferences in BC
  • BCCPAC membership and conference travel
  • Emergency supplies the school does not provide

Not eligible

  • Curricular materials and classroom items (textbooks, tablets, Chromebooks)
  • Fundraising costs, including concessions
  • Out-of-province travel without approval
  • Scholarships and bursaries
  • Repairs the district is responsible for
  • Items the district is responsible for (AV, PPE)
  • Goods or services for parents
  • PAC admin costs (office supplies, accounting, insurance)
  • Staff costs (luncheons, wages, professional development)
  • Cash prizes and gift cards
  • Past debts, loans, or interest
  • Covering a deficit in the general account
  • Donations to other groups

Other rules: no other money may be deposited into the gaming account; no cash withdrawals; cheques need two unrelated signers; e-transfers need written approval from two executive members with the purpose and maximum amount; money moves to the general account only to repay eligible expenses from the same fiscal year, with a reimbursement summary. Check the current PAC guidelines and conditions.
