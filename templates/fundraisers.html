How fundraisers work
  • Open a general-account transaction and choose the fundraiser beside its line item. Do this for the money in and for the costs, such as the supplier's bill.
  • For a deposit or a payment that covers more than one thing, split it across line items and give each part its own fundraiser.
  • A rule can choose the fundraiser when you import a bank file. See Assignment rules.
  • On a fundraiser's page, set a goal for the year: the net amount to raise.
  • Fundraisers show the same money a second way. The budget and the reports still count each part on its line item.
  • Gaming funds cannot go to a fundraiser. Record a licensed raffle or bingo as a licensed gaming event.
