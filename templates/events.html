How to record a licensed gaming event
  1. Add the event here with its licence number and class.
  2. Deposit all event revenue into the gaming account. Give the deposit the line item Licensed gaming event revenue, and choose the event on the transaction.
  3. Pay prizes and event costs from the gaming account. Use Licensed gaming event prizes or Licensed gaming event expenses, and choose the event. Attach the receipts.
  4. A donated prize costs nothing, so it has no transaction.
  5. The Gaming Account Summary Report then fills lines 6 to 13 and 19 to 26.

Your licence may also need its own event report. Check the licence conditions from the Gaming Policy and Enforcement Branch.
