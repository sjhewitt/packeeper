How hot lunch works
  1. Set the menu and the divisions (classes) under Menu and divisions.
  2. Set up payments, and share the sign-up link with families, under Payments and families.
  3. Add a period, add its lunch days with the items for each day, and open it.
  4. Parents add their children and order. Card payments are confirmed by Stripe or Helcim. Mark e-transfer and cash orders paid when the money arrives.
  5. After the deadline, close the period. Order from the caterer with the totals, and print a delivery sheet for each day.
  6. The money reaches the bank as Stripe or Helcim deposits, or as e-transfers. Import the bank file as usual, and give those deposits the hot lunch line item and fundraiser.
